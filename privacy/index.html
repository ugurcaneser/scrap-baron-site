Privacy Policy

This policy covers Scrap Baron: Scrapyard Tycoon on the App Store and Google Play, the web version at scrap-baron.ugurcaneser.workers.dev and these pages. The game is made and published by Uğurcan Eser, an individual developer in Türkiye, who is responsible for it (the “data controller” under the GDPR and Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK). Contact: ugurcaneser@icloud.com.

In short: Scrap Baron is free and collects no personal data. There are no accounts, purchases, ads, analytics, tracking or cookies, and the game sends nothing over the internet. Your saved game stays on your device. The only time we receive personal data is if you choose to email us.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your saved game: the day, the till, the stock, the yard, the plants, staff, contracts and the town.
  • Your profile: achievements (with the date each was earned), the kinds of cheating you have caught and the cosmetics you put up in the yard.
  • Settings: language, sound, ambience, vibration, Rıza Usta's tips, whether the tutorial is done and which help screens you have already seen.

All of this is stored in the local storage of the app (or of your browser for the web version). It never leaves your device by itself and never reaches us. It is stored only because the game needs it to work, so no consent banner is shown. It is deleted when you delete the app or clear this site's data in your browser; “New game” replaces the saved game. If your device backups are on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the operating system may include this data in your backup; those backups are Google's and Apple's services and we have no access to them.

The save code (“Move your save”)

The code on the “Move your save” screen is made on your device and contains your game, achievements and cosmetics, nothing else: no name, no device identifier, no location. Only you copy it and paste it wherever you like; the game never sends it anywhere. Anyone who has the code can load a copy of your game, so share it only where you want to.

Internet and permissions

The game plays offline and does not connect to any server: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs are built in. On Android the app declares two permissions: vibration (for the vibration setting) and internet (a default of the app framework, Capacitor, that the game does not use). On iPhone the game asks for no permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. The only links in the game (this policy and the support page) open in your browser.

The app stores

You download the app from the App Store or Google Play, which handle the download, updates and their own accounts under Apple's and Google's privacy policies. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers, Apple and Google show us aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs by country) and anonymous crash reports (device model, system version and the point in the code where the crash happened). We cannot tell from them who you are, and we use them only to fix problems.

The web version and these pages

The web version is served by Cloudflare, and these pages by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect the sites from abuse, and Cloudflare asks browsers to report failed connections to it (Network Error Logging). This happens under Cloudflare's and GitHub's privacy policies; we do not receive these logs and use no analytics, cookies or tracking. The web version stores your game in your browser's local storage, as described above. All its files, fonts included, come from the same address.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, your message and anything you choose to include (for example your device model, a save code or the diagnostic code from the game’s error screen). We use it only to answer you and to fix what you report, based on our legitimate interest in supporting the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). We do not add you to any list and do not share your message with anyone. Our mailbox is hosted by Apple (iCloud Mail), which may store it on servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. We delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the last message, unless we need it to establish or defend a legal claim.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included, and has no chat, sharing or user profiles. If a child under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country) emails us, we use the message only to reply and then delete it; parents can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK (Art. 11) you have the right to know whether we process data about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to data portability. Because the game sends us nothing, the only data we could hold about you is email you sent us; write to ugurcaneser@icloud.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr) or to the data protection authority where you live. The game data on your device is yours to delete at any time by deleting the app or clearing the site's data.

Changes

If a future version changes anything here (for example by adding an online feature), this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date before that version is released, and the stores' privacy labels will be updated with it.
